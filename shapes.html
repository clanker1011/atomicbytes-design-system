Shape sheet

Mid-century shapes

A working sheet of AtomicBytes SVGs — starbursts, orbits, boomerangs, blobs, and panels — for decoration, empty states, and print moments. Brand colors only. Steal the files from assets/shapes/.

Do Use them as stickers

  • Ink outline stays; fills are coral / mint / cobalt / yolk
  • Scale freely — they’re vectors
  • One or two accents per composition
  • Keep inspiration refs in assets/inspiration/ off the live marketing site

Don’t Stock habits

  • Trace or paste commercial clip-art sheets
  • Neon glows, gradients, or drop shadows
  • Mint/yolk as stroke color
  • Crowd a layout with every spark at once
